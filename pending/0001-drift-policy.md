# Drift policy

The five skills exist twice: the host-bound originals at
`~/.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` and the generalized copies in `skills/`.
The export was one-way and manual. Nothing detects divergence, and the two
cannot be diffed directly because the sanitization is a real transformation
(placeholders, deleted risk statements), not a rename.

Options when this first bites:

1. **Host is truth** — re-export and re-sanitize on demand; this repo is a
   snapshot that goes stale by design. Cheapest; accepts staleness.
2. **Repo is truth** — methodology lives here, hosts hold a bound copy
   generated by filling placeholders. Needs a binding step that does not
   exist yet.
3. **Split the artifact** — methodology in a shared file, host specifics in
   a separate binding file the skill reads. Cleanest, most work.

Do not hand-merge across the sanitization boundary — that is how a scrubbed
detail gets re-introduced into the published copy.
